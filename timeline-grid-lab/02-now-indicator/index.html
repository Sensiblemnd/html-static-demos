Now Indicator

A registered @property --now-position (a <percentage>) drives a vertical line's inset-inline-start. A @keyframes loop animates it left to right forever. Since there's no JavaScript to read the real clock, it simulates a day passing instead of tracking one. Respects prefers-reduced-motion.
