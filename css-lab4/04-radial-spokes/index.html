Radial Spokes

Eight items, evenly spaced around a circle at calc((360deg / sibling-count()) * sibling-index()) each. Add or remove an item from the markup and the spacing recomputes on its own, no manual angle math or a synced --count variable to update. Without support for the two functions, this falls back to a plain wrapped row.
